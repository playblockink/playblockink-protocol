# Playblock Whitepaper

## 1. Vision and mission

**Vision.** Make a token feel like a toy: simple to hold, fun to show, easy to explain.

**Mission.** Ship a small, honest, playful project on Solana. Keep the numbers public, keep the tone light, and
build tools around the brick one layer at a time.

Playblock is a community coin. It makes no promise of returns, and nothing in this document is financial advice.

## 3. Roadmap

**Phase 1: Base plate.** Brand identity, website, community groundwork, public market board.

**Phase 2: First layer.** Token launch, contract address published on the site, own-token row goes live on the board.

**Phase 3: The tower.** Tooling, collaborations, and community-led campaigns.

**Phase 4: Playground.** Small games and toys that put the brick to use.

Phases are ordered, not dated. Each one ships when it is ready.
